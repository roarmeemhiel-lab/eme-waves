What is the Electromagnetic Spectrum?

The electromagnetic (EM) spectrum is the entire range of all types of electromagnetic radiation — from radio waves (longest wavelength) to gamma rays (shortest wavelength).

📏

Wavelength & Frequency

Wavelength (λ) is the distance between wave peaks. Frequency (f) is the number of waves per second.

  • Radio waves: longest wavelength, lowest frequency
  • Gamma rays: shortest wavelength, highest frequency
  • They are inversely proportional: λ × f = c
⚡

Energy & Speed

Energy increases with frequency. All EM waves travel at the same speed in a vacuum.

  • Speed of light: c = 3 × 10⁸ m/s
  • Higher frequency = higher energy
  • Radio waves have the lowest energy
  • Gamma rays have the highest energy
📋

The 7 Types of EM Waves

  • Radio Waves — AM/FM radio, TV, cellphones
  • Microwaves — Ovens, WiFi, radar
  • Infrared — Remote controls, thermal cameras
  • Visible Light — The only part we can see!
  • Ultraviolet — Sunburn, sterilization
  • X-rays — Medical imaging, security
  • Gamma Rays — Cancer treatment, nuclear
🔑

Key Concepts

  • All EM waves are transverse waves
  • They don't need a medium to travel
  • Order from longest λ → shortest λ:
  • Radio → Micro → IR → Visible → UV → X-ray → Gamma
  • 🎯 Remember: "Raging Martians Invaded Venus Using X-ray Guns"
